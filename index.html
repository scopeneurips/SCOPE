A brief summary of the SCOPE framework and its contributions.

We present SCOPE (Simulated Controllable Open Playable Environments), a novel framework for game AI and world modeling. SCOPE enables controllable, open-ended simulation of interactive game environments, providing a powerful testbed for developing and evaluating game AI agents. Our approach demonstrates strong performance across diverse game scenarios, enabling rich and varied gameplay behaviors that were previously difficult to achieve.
